# MobileBuild

Builders for Apple, Android, cross-platform, JavaScript, and Bazel projects. Build outcomes include
the artifacts reported by the underlying tools, diagnostic counts, logs, and elapsed time.

## Topics

### Apple

- ``XcodeBuilder``
- ``XcodeBuildRequest``

### Android and JVM

- ``GradleBuilder``
- ``GradleProject``

### Cross-platform and other build systems

- ``FlutterBuilder``
- ``ReactNativeBuilder``
- ``KMPBuilder``
- ``SwiftPMBuilder``
- ``BazelBuilder``

### Outcomes and artifacts

- ``BuildOutcome``
- ``BuildArtifact``
- ``BuildFailure``
