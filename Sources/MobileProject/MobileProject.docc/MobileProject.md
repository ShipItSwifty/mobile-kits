# MobileProject

Read-only inspection of project files and Swift UI-test harnesses. Inspection does not execute
`Package.swift`, Gradle scripts, or Tuist manifests.

- ``RepositoryInspector``
- ``RepositoryInspection``
- ``FrameworkDetector``

### UI-test harness discovery

- ``UITestHarnessInspector``

- ``Evidence``
